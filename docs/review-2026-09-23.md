## Decisions

- Work order: CI/process unblock, then regressions and runtime bugs, build
  and toolchain, test coverage, remote security, target bring-up, and only
  then the remaining content hardening, performance and Team Arena.
- The open hardening PRs (#171, #184–#196, #211–#219) are paused until they
  are re-scoped against this order.
- Evidence goes in PR descriptions instead of per-step documents. Issues whose
  acceptance needs Mac OS 9 stay open with the `needs target test` label.
- The merge gate becomes current-head CI plus an approving independent
  adversarial reviewer agent on that exact head. CodeRabbit findings are
  addressed when it reviews a PR but do not block merging. Codex review
  credits are exhausted and CodeRabbit is throttled to one review per hour.
